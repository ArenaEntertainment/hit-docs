Your HIT Wallet shows two kinds of balance:

| Balance | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Cash | Your coins on each network shown in the app, for example Cash on Base, Cash on Solana and Cash on Tron. |
| Game | Your casino balance. It's the only balance you play with. |

## Add Funds

1. Open your HIT Wallet and pick a network.
2. Send a supported coin to the deposit address shown for that network.
3. Once it arrives, it shows in your Cash for that network.

You can also use the other [deposit methods](/markets/deposits).

## Play

Move Cash to Game, then play. When you're done, move Game back to Cash. See [Moving Funds](/markets/moving-funds).
