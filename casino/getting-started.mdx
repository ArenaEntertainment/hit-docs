Your HIT Wallet shows two kinds of balance:

| Balance | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Cash | Your coins on each network: Cash on Base (USDC on Base), Cash on Solana (USDC on Solana) and Cash on Tron (USDT on Tron). |
| Game | Your casino balance. It's the only balance you play with. |

## Add Funds

1. Click your wallet balance at the top of the page, then **Wallet**.
2. On the **Cash** tab, click **Deposit**.
3. Send a supported coin to the deposit address shown for the network you pick.
4. Once it arrives, it shows in your Cash for that network.

You can also use the other [deposit methods](/markets/deposits).

## Play

Move Cash to Game, then play. When you're done, move Game back to Cash. See [Moving Funds](/markets/moving-funds).
