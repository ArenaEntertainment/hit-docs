You can withdraw your Cash from Hit at any time to a wallet you control on the same network. Before you start, make sure you have a withdrawable Cash balance.

### How to Withdraw

1. Click your wallet balance at the top of the page, then **Wallet**.
2. On the **Cash** tab, click **Withdraw**. The button is disabled while your Cash balance is zero. You can also click **Withdraw** on your portfolio page.
3. Under **From**, pick the Cash you want to send: Cash on Base, Cash on Solana or Cash on Tron.
4. Enter the **Amount**, or use the **25%**, **50%** or **100%** shortcuts.
5. Enter the **Address** for that network (for example a Base address for Cash on Base), or click **Paste**, then confirm.

The least you can withdraw is $20.00. Fees and limits are shown in the app.

<Warning>
  **Important:** Double-check the address and network before confirming.
  - The address must be on the same network as the Cash you withdraw from. Sending to an address on a different network can result in permanent loss of funds.
  - Exercise caution when withdrawing directly to exchange wallets; not all exchanges credit deposits on every supported chain.
</Warning>

To withdraw Casino funds, first move them from Play to Cash. See [Moving Funds](/markets/moving-funds).

<Info>
  **Limit Orders:** If you have resting limit orders, withdrawing funds may
  cause some orders to be cancelled if fulfilling them would exceed your
  available liquid balance.
</Info>
