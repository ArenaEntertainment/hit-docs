Your HIT Wallet holds your Cash (your coins on each network) and your Game balance (for Casino). You trade on Markets with your Cash on Base. {/* CONFIRM(harry): are the HIT Wallet buttons labelled "Swap to Game" and "Withdraw from Game"? */}

## Cash to Game

1. Open your HIT Wallet.
2. Choose **Swap to Game** and enter the amount.
3. Confirm. The minimum and any fees are shown in the app.

## Game to Cash

1. Open your HIT Wallet.
2. Choose **Withdraw from Game**, then pick the network and amount.
3. Confirm. Minimums, fees and limits are shown in the app.

You can only move the withdrawable part of your Game balance. Bonus funds stay in Game.

## Automatic Moves

If you accepted automatic moves in the app, supported coins that arrive in your wallet are moved into your Cash for you. They only go to your own Cash, never to Game.

## Statements

Your HIT Wallet history lists your money movements. You can download a statement for a period you choose as CSV or PDF. {/* CONFIRM(harry): is wallet history with CSV and PDF statements on for every prod user? */}
