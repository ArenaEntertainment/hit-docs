---
title: Moving Funds
description: Move funds between Cash, Casino and Portfolio in your HIT Wallet
---

Your HIT Wallet holds your Cash (your coins on each network) and your Casino balance. You trade on Markets with your Cash on Base. Other coins in your wallet show in **Portfolio**, where you can sell them to Cash or swap them to Casino.

To open it, click your wallet balance at the top of the page, then **Wallet**. It has the tabs **Summary**, **Casino**, **Cash** and **Portfolio**.

## Cash to Casino

1. On the **Cash** tab, click **Swap**.
2. Set **From** to the Cash you want to move (for example Cash on Base) and **To** to Casino.
3. Under **Choose an Amount**, enter the amount (or use **25%**, **50%** or **100%**) and confirm. The minimum and any fees are shown in the app.

## Casino to Cash

1. On the **Cash** tab, click **Swap**, then use the arrow button to flip the direction to Casino to Cash. You can also click **Swap with Cash** on the **Casino** tab.
2. Under **Choose an Amount**, enter the amount and confirm. Minimums, fees and limits are shown in the app.

You can only move the withdrawable part of your Casino balance. Bonus funds stay in your Casino balance.

## Between Cash Networks

On the **Cash** tab, click **Swap**, set **From** to one Cash line and **To** to another (for example Cash on Base to Cash on Solana), enter the amount and confirm.

## From Portfolio

The **Portfolio** tab ("Your crypto, in your wallet.") shows the other coins in your wallet, with **Deposit** and **Swap** buttons. From Portfolio you can sell coins to Cash or swap them to Casino.

On the **Casino** tab, **Swap with Portfolio** tops up your Casino balance with coins you hold in Portfolio, without selling them to Cash first.

## Automatic Moves

Automatic moves are a one-time prompt in the app, not a wallet setting. Once you accept it, USDC and USDT that arrive in your wallet are moved into your Cash for you. Only these dollar coins are moved. Anything else stays in **Portfolio**. Automatic moves only go to your own Cash, never to Casino.

## Recent Moves

Your recent moves are listed under **Earlier** in each tab.

To move funds out of Hit, see [Withdrawals](/markets/withdrawals).
