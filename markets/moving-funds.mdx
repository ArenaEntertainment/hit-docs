---
title: Moving Funds
description: Move funds between Cash and Play in your HIT Wallet
---

Your HIT Wallet holds your Cash (your coins on each network) and your Play balance (for Casino). You trade on Markets with your Cash on Base. If a deposit on Base shows in **Portfolio**, move it to Cash before you trade or swap it to Play.

To open it, click your wallet balance at the top of the page, then **Wallet**. It has the tabs **Summary**, **Play**, **Cash** and **Portfolio**.

## Cash to Play

1. On the **Cash** tab, click **Swap**.
2. Set **From** to Cash and **To** to Play.
3. Under **Choose an Amount**, enter the amount and confirm. The minimum and any fees are shown in the app.

## Play to Cash

1. On the **Cash** tab, click **Swap**, then use the arrow button to flip the direction to Play to Cash. You can also click **Swap with Cash** on the **Play** tab.
2. Under **Choose an Amount**, enter the amount and confirm. Minimums, fees and limits are shown in the app.

You can only move the withdrawable part of your Play balance. Bonus funds stay in Play.

## Automatic Moves

If you accepted automatic moves in the app, supported coins that arrive in your wallet are moved into your Cash for you. They only go to your own Cash, never to Play.

## Recent Moves

Your recent moves are listed under **Earlier** in each tab.

To move funds out of Hit, see [Withdrawals](/markets/withdrawals).
