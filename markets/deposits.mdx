To start trading on Hit, you'll need to deposit funds into your account. Before you start, make sure you have [created an account](/markets/signing-up).

<Info>
  Deposits are crypto only. There is no card, bank or other fiat option. You
  send coins to your HIT Wallet address on one of the supported networks.
</Info>

### Deposit Methods

The deposit screen shows a network picker, plus a QR code and wallet address for the network you pick. The supported networks are Base, Solana, Ethereum, Tron, BNB Chain, Robinhood Chain, Arbitrum, Optimism and Polygon.

#### QR Code

Select a network and token to generate a deposit address (shown as a QR code). Any supported funds sent to this address are automatically converted to USDC on the Base network and credited to your HIT Wallet. Review the estimated fees shown in the app before confirming your transfer.

#### Direct Transfer

Send a supported coin to the deposit address shown in your HIT Wallet for that network. Supported networks and coins are shown in the app. Funds sent on Base go to your Cash on Base, the balance you trade with on Markets. If the app shows them in **Portfolio** instead, move them to Cash before you trade or swap them to Play.

<Warning>
  **Important:** Only send a coin the app lists, on the network you picked, to the address shown for that network.
  - Sending a different token, or using a different network, can result in permanent loss of funds.
  - Funds sent to any other address cannot be recovered by Hit support.
</Warning>

### How to Deposit

Open the deposit flow from either of these entry points:

- Click your wallet balance at the top of any page, select **Wallet**, then click **Deposit** on the **Summary** or **Cash** tab.
- From your portfolio page, click the **Deposit** button near the top.

Then:

1. Pick a network.
2. Send coins to the address shown, or scan the QR code. Click **Copy Address** to copy the address.
3. Start trading once your deposit is confirmed in your account.
