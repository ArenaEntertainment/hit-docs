To start trading on Hit, you'll need to deposit funds into your account. Before you start, make sure you have [created an account](/markets/signing-up).

<Info>
  Deposits are crypto only. There is no card, bank or other fiat option. You
  send coins to your HIT Wallet address on one of the supported networks.
</Info>

### Deposit Screen

The deposit screen says "Send any token to any of these networks." Pick a network, then send coins to the wallet address shown for it, or scan the QR code. Click **Copy Address** to copy the address. The supported networks are Base, Solana, Ethereum, Tron, BNB Chain, Robinhood Chain, Arbitrum, Optimism and Polygon.

### Where Your Coins Land

- Coins sent on Ethereum, BNB Chain, Robinhood Chain, Arbitrum, Optimism or Polygon land in your own wallet on that network and show in **Portfolio**.
- USDC and USDT are moved into your Cash for you once you accept the one-time automatic moves prompt. Only these dollar coins are moved automatically. Nothing else is converted.
- Anything else stays in **Portfolio**, where you can sell it to Cash or swap it to Casino. See [Moving Funds](/markets/moving-funds).

You trade on Markets with your Cash on Base.

<Warning>
  **Important:** Any token you send on a supported network arrives in your wallet, but Hit only shows and lets you sell coins it lists or that are verified. If you send an obscure token, it may arrive and you may not be able to sell it.
  - Send only on the network you picked, to the address shown for that network. Using a different network can result in permanent loss of funds.
  - Funds sent to any other address cannot be recovered by Hit support.
</Warning>

### How to Deposit

Open the deposit flow from either of these entry points:

- Click your wallet balance at the top of any page, select **Wallet**, then click **Deposit** on the **Summary** or **Cash** tab.
- From your portfolio page, click the **Deposit** button near the top.

Then:

1. Pick a network.
2. Send coins to the address shown, or scan the QR code. Click **Copy Address** to copy the address.
3. Start trading once your deposit is confirmed in your account.
