title: Signing Up
description: Get started with Hit in minutes
---

Click the **Login** button and choose your sign-up method:

- **Email Sign Up** – Create an account with your email address.
- **Google Login** – Sign up instantly with your Google account.
- **Connect Wallet** – Use an existing crypto wallet.

### Wallet Setup

When you sign up, Hit creates your HIT Wallet for you. Hit covers network fees, so you never need ETH to trade. You can export your private key from account settings.

<Warning>
  Keep your private key secret: anyone who has it controls your HIT Wallet and its funds.
</Warning>

<Info>
  **Mobile Trading:** For the best mobile experience, we recommend using Email
  or Google sign-up rather than wallet connection.
</Info>
