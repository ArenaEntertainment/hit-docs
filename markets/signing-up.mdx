Click the **Login** button and choose your sign-up method:

- **Email Sign Up** – Create an account with your email address.
- **Google Login** – Sign up instantly with your Google account.
- **Connect Wallet** – Use an existing crypto wallet.

### Wallet Setup

Once you sign up, Hit automatically creates your HIT wallet. It's your own self-custodial wallet: it holds your USDC and shares, it's the address you deposit to, and it sends your withdrawals.

Hit covers all network fees, so you never need ETH to trade and there's nothing to approve or set up.

You're ready to use the site once your **HIT Wallet** appears on your [Profile](https://www.hit.com/profile) page.

You can export your wallet's private key anytime from your account settings.

<Warning>
  Anyone with your private key controls your wallet and all the funds in it. Never share it, and never enter it on a site you don't trust.
</Warning>

<Info>
  **Mobile Trading:** For the best mobile experience, we recommend using Email
  or Google sign-up rather than wallet connection.
</Info>
